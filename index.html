Privacy Policy for BladeSync

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the BladeSync application, created by Coding Minds.

1. Sensor Data & Hardware Connections

BladeSync connects directly to external sensor hardware via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). The application collects motion metrics including rotational speed, vertical jump height, acceleration, and exit velocity solely for performance tracking.

2. Storage and Data Transmission

Telemetry metrics are stored locally on your device and synced securely via encrypted HTTPS to our cloud servers for historical analytics. We do not collect or store your precise GPS coordinates, personal home address, or unencrypted biometric data.

3. Guest Mode

BladeSync includes a Guest Mode allowing users to explore sample motion datasets without establishing an account or transmitting personal information.
