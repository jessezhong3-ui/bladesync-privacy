Welcome to BladeSync. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This document outlines how we handle your information and the terms governing your use of the application.

1. About the App

BladeSync is designed for figure skaters. who are looking for quantitative data instead of relying on plain eyesight.

2. Accounts and Information Collected

To access our services, accounts require: an email and a password. A guest mode is included for temporary data collection.

3. Device Permissions (Camera, Microphone, Location)

Our app handles hardware permissions as follows:

  • Camera & Microphone: A feature for the user to use. For skaters who want to record their jumps and compare it with the data measured.
  • Location: Not used.

4. Data Storage and Content

  • Stored User Data: We store profile settings, high scores, activity logs using Google Firebase and local device storage.
  • User-Submitted Content: Any content you submit [videos, photos, etc.] can be seen by only you and is retained for as long as your account is active.
